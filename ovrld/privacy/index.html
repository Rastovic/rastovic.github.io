OVRLD Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Srpski ispod

The short version. Everything you log in OVRLD stays on your phone. There is no account, no cloud and no server of ours. OVRLD does not even have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your training anywhere.

What OVRLD stores, and where

Your profile (name, date of birth, sex if you choose to give it, height, body-weight entries), your exercises, workouts, weekly plans, logged sets, cardio sessions (time, distance and, if you type it, average heart rate), ratings and notes are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) never receive them. We have no server that could receive them.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

  • Backups. When you tap Export in OVRLD, the app makes one backup file and hands it to your phone's share menu. Where it goes after that (Drive, email, a folder) is up to you. Restore reads a file you pick.
  • Your phone's own backup. If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include OVRLD's data in your Google account backup, so it comes back on a new phone. That is a feature of Android, run by Google under your account. We cannot see it.

We don't count these as us collecting your data. They are copies you or your phone make for you.

Home-screen widget

If you add the OVRLD widget, it shows today's workout name, its exercises and target weights, or "Rest", on your home screen. Anyone who can see your home screen can see that. Remove the widget to hide it.

Permissions

  • Notifications are used only for the rest timer: a silent countdown while you rest between sets, and one alert when the rest is over.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional) lets that alert arrive on the second with the screen off. OVRLD works without it.
  • Vibration is used for the rest-over buzz and for button feedback.
  • Run at startup lets Android restore a pending rest alert after the phone restarts.
  • Prevent sleeping and view network connections are listed because the Android component that updates the home-screen widget declares them. OVRLD makes no network connections.

OVRLD does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or your files (except a backup file you pick yourself).

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

This version of OVRLD shows no ads and has no analytics, crash reporting or other tracking. It does not sell data, and it has no data to sell. If a later version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be stated here.

Not medical advice

OVRLD is a training log. Its suggestions (for example, adding weight when every set reached the top of your rep range) are simple rules applied to what you logged, not medical or professional advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training programme if you have any health concerns.

Children

OVRLD is not directed at children under 16.

Deleting your data

Delete a workout, cardio session or body-weight entry inside the app, or uninstall OVRLD to remove everything it stored. There is nothing stored with us to delete. Backup files you made yourself stay wherever you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

OVRLD — politika privatnosti

Važi od 12. oktobra 2026.

Ukratko. Sve što upišeš u OVRLD ostaje na tvom telefonu. Nema naloga, nema oblaka i nema našeg servera. OVRLD nema ni dozvolu za internet, pa tvoje treninge ne može nikome poslati.

Šta OVRLD čuva i gdje

Profil (ime, datum rođenja, pol ako ga uneseš, visina, unosi tjelesne težine), vježbe, treninzi, sedmični planovi, upisane serije, kardio (vrijeme, razdaljina i, ako ga upišeš, prosječan puls), ocjene i bilješke čuvaju se samo u privatnom prostoru aplikacije na tvom uređaju. Mi (programer) ih nikad ne dobijamo, niti imamo server na koji bi mogli stići.

Šta napušta telefon, i to samo kad ti odlučiš

  • Rezervna kopija — na Export aplikacija pravi jedan fajl i predaje ga meniju za dijeljenje. Gdje dalje ide, zavisi od tebe. Restore čita fajl koji ti izabereš.
  • Rezervna kopija telefona — ako je uključena u podešavanjima telefona, Android može uključiti podatke OVRLD-a u rezervnu kopiju tvog Google naloga. To je funkcija Androida; mi je ne vidimo.

Vidžet na početnom ekranu

Ako dodaš vidžet, on prikazuje današnji trening, vježbe i ciljne težine, ili "Rest". Vidi ga svako ko vidi tvoj početni ekran. Ukloni vidžet da ga sakriješ.

Dozvole

  • Obavještenja — samo za tajmer odmora između serija.
  • Alarmi i podsjetnici (nije obavezno) — da obavještenje o kraju odmora stigne tačno na vrijeme.
  • Vibracija — za kraj odmora i dodir dugmadi.
  • Pokretanje pri uključivanju — da se odmor na čekanju vrati nakon restarta telefona.
  • Sprečavanje mirovanja i pregled mrežnih veza — navodi ih Android komponenta koja osvježava vidžet. OVRLD ne pravi mrežne veze.

Bez reklama, analitike i praćenja

Ova verzija nema reklame, analitiku ni praćenje. Ako kasnija verzija doda reklame, ova politika biće ažurirana prije te verzije.

Nije medicinski savjet

OVRLD je dnevnik treninga. Njegovi prijedlozi su jednostavna pravila primijenjena na ono što upišeš, a ne medicinski ni stručni savjet.

Brisanje podataka

Obriši trening, kardio ili unos težine u aplikaciji, ili deinstaliraj OVRLD. Kod nas nema ničega za brisanje.
