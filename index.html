The promise

Every app I ship
0accounts. You never sign up, so there is nothing to sign into or leak.
0servers of ours. Your entries live in the app's storage on your phone.
1file, when you want a backup. You make it, you keep it, you restore it.

OVRLD and LockIn have no internet access at all. Tideline shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, and those never see what you log. Each app's privacy policy says exactly what it does.
