LockIn Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

In short: LockIn has no account and no server. Your tasks, focus sessions and app usage are stored only on your phone and never sent to us. The one part that uses the internet is advertising, provided by Google AdMob, described below.

Who we are

LockIn is an Android app made by Jelena Rastović, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: CONTACT-EMAIL.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in LockIn is stored in a database on your device:

  • tasks, projects, goals, day plans and what you ticked off;
  • focus sessions, minutes earned and spent, savings and treats;
  • the apps you chose to watch, and how long each was open;
  • your settings, theme and the one-time tips you have dismissed.

We do not collect, receive or have access to any of it. There is no LockIn account, no cloud sync and no analytics.

Usage access

If you grant usage access, LockIn reads how long the apps you chose to watch were open, using Android's usage statistics. This is how it counts the minutes you spend. It cannot see what you do inside those apps. The data stays on your phone. You can revoke usage access at any time in Android Settings; planning and the focus timer keep working without it.

Blocking and the accessibility service

Blocking is off unless you turn it on. When you turn it on, LockIn asks you to enable its accessibility service, after explaining what it does. The service only receives notice of which app has come to the front, so it can cover a watched app once your minutes run out. It does not read what is on screen, what you type or any window content, and nothing it observes leaves your phone. You can turn it off in LockIn or in Android Settings at any time.

Notifications and alarms

LockIn uses notifications for a running focus block and an alarm at its end. They are created and delivered on your phone.

Home screen widgets

The widgets show today's plan and your balance. LockIn stores a copy of what they display in the app's private storage on your phone so they can draw without the app open. Text typed into the capture widget is kept there until LockIn next opens and turns it into a task.

Backups and exports

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include LockIn's data in your device backup, stored by Google and governed by your Google account settings. We cannot access it.
  • Backup file and spreadsheet export. These are created only when you ask, and saved or shared wherever you choose.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

LockIn shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, which may include:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

This information is sent to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

None of your LockIn data - tasks, usage, sessions or balance - is shared with Google or used for advertising.

Your ad choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, LockIn asks for your consent before ads are loaded, using Google's consent tool. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Elsewhere → Privacy choices.
  • On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Children

LockIn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you control it entirely. Uninstalling LockIn, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes everything it holds. Backup files you exported are yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
