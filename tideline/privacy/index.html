Tideline Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Srpski ispod

The short version. Everything you log in Tideline stays on your phone. There is no account, no cloud and no server of ours. The only thing that talks to the internet is Google AdMob, which shows the banner ads. It never receives anything you log.

What Tideline stores, and where

Your markers, entries, notes, diary, photos, reports and profiles are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) never receive them. We have no server that could receive them.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

  • Backups. When you make a backup, Tideline writes a file to a place you pick. Where it goes after that is up to you.
  • Exports and shared reports (CSV, a text file for an AI chat, a PDF for a doctor) are made only when you ask for them and go only where you send them. Diary text and notes are left out unless you include them yourself. Private entries and photos are never included.

We don't count these as us collecting your data. They are files you make and send yourself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows banner ads on two screens that contain none of your entries: Settings and the marker list. The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived),
  • interactions with the ad (views and taps), and
  • diagnostic information about how the ad performed.

Tideline sends Google nothing you log. That includes your entries and your marker names. Tideline does not use interest-based ad topics. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad loads. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Google → Ads.

Permissions

  • Camera is used only to take a photo you attach to an entry. Photos stay on your device.
  • Notifications and alarms are used only for the one evening reminder, and only if you turn it on.
  • Biometrics are used for the app lock. Your phone checks your fingerprint or face. Tideline never sees it.
  • Internet is used only for the ads.

No analytics, no tracking of your entries

Tideline has no analytics and no crash-reporting service of its own, and no other tracking. It does not sell data, and it has no data to sell.

Not a medical device

Tideline is a personal record. It shows what you log. It does not give medical advice or reach conclusions about your health.

Children

Tideline is not directed at children under 16. Anyone keeping a record for a child should do so from their own phone.

Deleting your data

Delete a profile in Settings, or uninstall the app. Either removes that data from the app. There is nothing stored with us to delete. Backup files you made yourself stay wherever you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Tideline — politika privatnosti

Važi od 25. septembra 2026.

Ukratko. Sve što upišeš u Tideline ostaje na tvom telefonu. Nema naloga, nema oblaka i nema našeg servera. Na internet ide samo Google AdMob, koji prikazuje banner reklame. On nikad ne dobija ništa od onoga što upišeš.

Šta Tideline čuva i gdje

Markeri, unosi, bilješke, dnevnik, fotografije, izvještaji i profili čuvaju se samo u privatnom prostoru aplikacije na tvom uređaju. Mi (programer) ih nikad ne dobijamo, niti imamo server na koji bi mogli stići.

Šta napušta telefon, i to samo kad ti odlučiš

  • Rezervne kopije — fajl se pravi na mjestu koje ti izabereš. Gdje dalje ide, zavisi od tebe.
  • Izvozi i podijeljeni izvještaji (CSV, tekst za AI chat, PDF za doktora) prave se samo na tvoj zahtjev i idu samo tamo gdje ih ti pošalješ. Dnevnik i bilješke su isključeni ako ih sama ne uključiš. Privatni unosi i fotografije nikad ne idu.

Reklame (Google AdMob)

Besplatna verzija prikazuje banner reklame na dva ekrana bez tvojih unosa: Podešavanja i lista markera. Za prikaz i mjerenje reklama i sprečavanje prevara Google može prikupiti:

  • reklamni ID uređaja,
  • IP adresu (iz nje se može odrediti približna lokacija),
  • interakcije s reklamom i
  • dijagnostičke podatke o reklami.

Tideline Google-u ne šalje ništa od onoga što upišeš, uključujući unose i imena markera. Tideline ne koristi teme interesovanja za reklame.

Tvoji izbori:

  • U EEA, Velikoj Britaniji i Švajcarskoj pristanak se traži prije prve reklame. Odgovor možeš promijeniti u Podešavanja → Izbori privatnosti.
  • Reklamni ID možeš resetovati ili obrisati u podešavanjima telefona, pod Google → Oglasi.

Dozvole

  • Kamera — samo za fotografiju uz unos. Fotografije ostaju na uređaju.
  • Obavještenja i alarmi — samo za večernji podsjetnik, i samo ako ga uključiš.
  • Biometrija — za zaključavanje aplikacije. Otisak ili lice provjerava telefon, a Tideline ih nikad ne vidi.
  • Internet — samo za reklame.

Nije medicinski uređaj

Tideline je lični zapis. Prikazuje ono što upišeš. Ne daje medicinske savjete i ne izvodi zaključke o zdravlju.

Brisanje podataka

Obriši profil u Podešavanjima ili deinstaliraj aplikaciju. Kod nas nema ničega za brisanje.
